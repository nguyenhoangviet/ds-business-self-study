🎯 What You Will Learn

📊 Data-Driven Decision Making

Move beyond spreadsheets; know when to trust your data and how to act on it.

📐 Statistical Thinking

Understand distributions, confidence, correlation vs. causation, and avoiding bias.

🔢 Regression & Prediction

Linear regression, forecasting, and building predictive models for business outcomes.

📉 Data Visualization

Create compelling charts and dashboards; communicate insights to non-technical stakeholders.

🗄️ Data Curation & Quality

Identify bad data, missing values, and how to clean and prepare datasets.

🤖 Intro to Machine Learning

Conceptual overview of classification, clustering, and when to use ML in business.

💬 Speaking Data Science

Communicate with data teams; understand R/Python syntax at a conceptual level.

📅 4-Week Study Schedule

Pace: 4–5 hours per week. Follow the Harvard course pace exactly — one phase per week.
Week 1
Foundations — Thinking with Data
What is data science? Types of data, business questions, data ethics, and the analytics pipeline.
Week 2
Statistics & Visualization
Descriptive statistics, distributions, correlation, hypothesis testing basics, and chart types.
Week 3
Regression, Forecasting & Prediction
Linear regression, R-squared, residuals, time series, and forecasting for business planning.
Week 4
Machine Learning & Storytelling
Classification, clustering, A/B testing, and building a data-driven business narrative.

🏁 Capstone Project (Replaces Harvard Assignment)

Goal: Apply everything you've learned to a real dataset and write a 1-page data-driven business recommendation.

Step 1 — Pick a Dataset

Choose a real business dataset from a free source.

FREE Kaggle Datasets FREE World Bank Open Data FREE Google Dataset Search

Step 2 — Analyze It

Run descriptive stats + at least one regression. Use R (Posit Cloud) or Excel.

TOOL Posit Cloud (RStudio) TOOL Google Sheets (free)

Step 3 — Visualize & Present

Build at least 3 charts. Write a 1-page recommendation as if presenting to a manager.

TOOL Tableau Public TOOL Canva Presentation
